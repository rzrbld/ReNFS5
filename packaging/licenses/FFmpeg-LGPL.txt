The port embeds a minimal, unmodified-source FFmpeg subset (LGPL-2.1-or-later)
to decode the game's EAMAD movies: libavformat (ea demuxer), libavcodec (eamad
video decoder, adpcm_ea_r1 audio decoder) and libavutil. No swscale, no encoders.
FFmpeg is linked statically into the port binary.

Licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License v2.1 or later; the license
text is in LGPL-2.1.txt. Relinking is possible: the port's build scripts and
full source are available in the project repository, and the static FFmpeg
archives are rebuilt from unmodified upstream sources by
scripts/build_ffmpeg_mini.sh.
